# Code of Conduct

Codeum is made for people who are learning to program, often without a computer and often alone.
That sets the tone here.

## The rules

- **Be patient with beginners.** "Read the docs" and "obviously you should know that" are not
  answers. If a question looks basic, the app probably explained something badly — that is a bug report.
- **Talk about the problem, not the person.** Disagree with an idea as much as you like; insults,
  mockery and personal attacks are not welcome.
- **No spam, no advertising, no off-topic promotion.**
- **Do not post other people's private data**, and be careful with your own: an issue is public, so
  remove tokens, keys, passwords and server addresses from logs and screenshots before posting.
- **Any language is fine.** Write in the language you are comfortable with — the app speaks ten of
  them, and a translated report is better than no report.

## If a rule is broken

Comments that break these rules may be edited or deleted, and repeat offenders may be blocked from
the repository. Decisions are made by the repository owner.

To report a problem with someone's behaviour, open an issue, or use GitHub's own
[report abuse](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) form if it should stay private.
